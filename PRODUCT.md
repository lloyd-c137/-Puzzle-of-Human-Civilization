# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One student performing a supervised classroom chemistry experiment. The student needs a clear, step-by-step workflow that can be followed at the bench and reviewed later by the student or teacher.

## Product Purpose

Provide a practical interactive companion for the Liquid Type Test Experiment. The tool guides the student through safety checks, sample setup, three measurement trials, density calculations, candidate comparison, conclusion writing, and record review. Experiment records must be real and durable in the browser rather than temporary interface state.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, clear. The interface should reduce cognitive load at the bench, make safety decisions visible, and explain what each input means without sounding promotional or childish.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard clutter, decorative science imagery, unexplained technical controls, hidden calculations, ambiguous save states, and interfaces that encourage a student to continue after a safety requirement fails.

## Design Principles

- Safety gates the workflow: unsafe or incomplete setup stops progression.
- One decision at a time: each stage presents only the instructions and fields needed now.
- Show the calculation: derived values remain inspectable and traceable to entered measurements.
- Save honestly: drafts, completed records, database status, and export state are visible.
- Review supports learning: saved trials and conclusions remain readable after the experiment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target keyboard-friendly, high-contrast, readable controls with explicit labels, visible focus states, semantic headings, status text that does not rely on color alone, and reduced-motion support.
