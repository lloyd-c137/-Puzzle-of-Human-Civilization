# Math Learning Journal

这是一个持续更新的数学学习记录，用来建立能够支持物理、化学和日常判断的数学基础。重点不是只记住公式，而是理解数量之间的关系、清楚地表达步骤，并能检查答案是否合理。

## Final learning goal

完成这一阶段后，我应该能够：

- 熟练处理整数、分数、小数、百分数和负数；
- 使用代数式、方程和比例表达问题；
- 读懂坐标、表格、图像和简单函数；
- 使用几何和测量公式解决实际问题；
- 进行单位换算、科学记数法和数量级估算；
- 计算并解释平均数、范围、比例和简单概率；
- 写出可复查的解题过程，并主动检查单位、符号和答案的合理性。

## How I will study

每次学习尽量完成以下循环：

1. 用自己的话解释一个概念。
2. 看一个完整例题，标出每一步的理由。
3. 独立完成一到三道相近题目。
4. 记录错误，不只记录正确答案。
5. 把这个概念连接到物理、化学或现实中的一个问题。

### Study note — Fuel cost word problem

**Problem:** Roselyn drives \(150\) km. Her fuel efficiency is \(6\) km/L, and fuel costs \(\$0.60\) per liter. She starts with \(20\) L and buys more gas if needed.

**Step 1 — Find total fuel used**

$$
\text{fuel used} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{fuel efficiency}}
= \frac{150\text{ km}}{6\text{ km/L}}
= 25\text{ L}
$$

**Step 2 — Find the cost of all fuel used**

$$
\text{total cost} = 25\text{ L} \times \$0.60/\text{L} = \$15
$$

She starts with \(20\) L, so she buys \(25-20=5\) additional liters. The additional purchase costs \(5\times\$0.60=\$3\), but the question asks for the cost of **all** fuel used, including the starting fuel, so the answer is **\(\boxed{\$15}\)**.

**Important distinction:** Yes, Roselyn only needs to **buy 5 L**, costing \(\boxed{\$3}\) during the trip. The \(\$15\) answer treats the starting 20 L as fuel that also has a cost, so it calculates the value of all 25 L used. If the question asked “How much more money does she spend along the way?”, the answer would be \(\boxed{\$3}\).

**What was not needed:** Average speed is irrelevant because the question asks for fuel cost, not travel time.

**General pattern:**

$$
\text{total fuel} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{kilometers per liter}}
$$

$$
\text{total fuel cost} = \text{total liters} \times \text{cost per liter}
$$

### Study note — Rice price and ratio word problem

**Step 1 — Find the total rice needed**

$$
10\text{ servings} \times 75\text{ g/serving} = 750\text{ g}
$$

**Step 2 — Find the rice price per gram**

$$
\text{price per gram} = \frac{\$4.50}{750\text{ g}} = \$0.006/\text{g}
$$

Decimal-place check: \(\$0.06\) per gram would make the total cost \(750\times\$0.06=\$45\), which is too large. Therefore the decimal must be one place farther to the left.

**Step 3 — Use the rice-to-spice ratio as a separate check**

The ratio is \(15:1\), so 15 grams of rice require 1 gram of spice:

$$
\text{spice needed} = \frac{750\text{ g rice}}{15} = 50\text{ g spice}
$$

David has 40 g, so he needs 10 g more spice. This ratio information is not needed to calculate the price of rice per gram.

### Study note — Engagement per writer

To compare engagement per writer, first find total engagement for the month, then divide by the number of writers.

**Definition 1 — Average likes per writer**

$$
\text{likes per writer}
= \frac{\text{posts} \times \text{average likes per post}}{\text{writers}}
$$

Website A:

$$
\frac{110\times3500}{5}=77{,}000\text{ likes per writer}
$$

Website B:

$$
\frac{200\times3500}{11}\approx63{,}636\text{ likes per writer}
$$

**Definition 2 — Average comments per writer**

$$
\text{comments per writer}
= \frac{\text{posts} \times \text{average comments per post}}{\text{writers}}
$$

Website A:

$$
\frac{110\times500}{5}=11{,}000\text{ comments per writer}
$$

Website B:

$$
\frac{200\times450}{11}\approx8{,}182\text{ comments per writer}
$$

Website A is higher according to both definitions, so the two definitions do **not** have opposite results. The correct conclusion is: **Yes, according to both definitions, Website A has more user engagement per writer.**
